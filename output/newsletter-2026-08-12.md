  * 1877 -- Thomas Edison completes work
    on the model that becomes the
    phonograph. Popular history puts the
    date here. The patent came later.
    First playback: a short nursery rhyme
    pressed into tinfoil. The room heard
    a human voice come back from a
    machine and the century changed its
    mind about what memory could be.

  * 1877 -- Same year, same sky:
    astronomer Asaph Hall discovers
    Deimos, one of Mars's two moons, at
    the U.S. Naval Observatory. A small
    rock with a big name. Proof that
    looking carefully is still a form of
    travel.

  * 1898 -- A peace protocol is signed
    ending the Spanish-American conflict.
    The same day, Hawaii is formally
    annexed to the United States. Two
    large decisions sharing one calendar
    square. History loves a crowded day.

  * 1960 -- Echo 1A launches: NASA's
    first successful communications
    satellite. A balloon in orbit that
    bounced signals across the planet.
    The ancestor of every message that
    has to travel farther than a shout.

  * 1981 -- IBM puts the Personal Computer
    Model 5150 into stores. About $1,500.
    No disk drives in the base fantasy.
    Forty-five years ago today, a beige
    box walked into American life and
    never left. You are reading a distant
    cousin of that moment.

     Wednesday Desk -- ON-DEVICE & HARDWARE
     "The brain that stays in your hand"
= = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = = =

  THE BIG SHIFT IN ONE SENTENCE
  Cloud AI made machines talk. On-device AI
  is trying to make machines act without
  asking the internet for permission every
  time.

  WHAT "ON-DEVICE" ACTUALLY MEANS
  A model that runs on the phone's own
  silicon. No round-trip for every token.
  Lower latency. Works on a plane. Costs
  zero per call after you own the hardware.
  The trade: smaller context than a giant
  cloud model, and it needs careful tool
  design so it does not invent actions.

  THE HARDWARE LAYER -- TENSOR G6
  Google's Pixel 11 chip is the clearest
  consumer example shipping today. Claims
  versus Tensor G5 at launch include:
  - about 25 percent faster web browsing
  - about 15 percent quicker app launches
  - about 50 percent more TPU compute
  - on-device AI tasks up to about 3.5x
    faster while using up to about 3.5x
    less energy

  The interesting part is not the press-
  release percentages. It is the split
  brain design: a heavy TPU path for big
  Gemini work, plus lighter paths for the
  always-on background jobs that used to
  drain a battery if you ran them wrong.

  THE MODEL LAYER -- GEMINI NANO v3
  Pixel 11 is the reference device for the
  latest on-device Gemini Nano. Reported
  shape of the stack:
  - runs fully on-device through Android
    AICore
  - roughly 1.8B to 3.25B parameters in
    the on-device family
  - thousands of tokens of local context
  - sub-second feel for many tasks
  - works offline

  The leap from earlier Nano versions is
  agent plumbing:
  1) on-device function calling
  2) structured JSON-style output

  In plain language: the phone model can
  decide "I need Tool X with these
  arguments," hand your app a clean action
  object, then wait for the app to do the
  real-world step. That is the difference
  between a summarizer and a junior agent.

  THE SOFTWARE LAYER -- FROM CHAT TO ACT
  Google is pitching Gemini Intelligence as
  proactive help across messages, calendar,
  maps, and camera. Features in the Pixel
  pitch include Gemini Live, Magic Cue,
  Circle to Search, Live Translate, and
  Call Assist. Availability still varies by
  country and language. Some higher usage
  needs a subscription.

  The strategic point: Pixel is trying to
  win by stacking chip + OS + model + apps
  as one product. Apple has a powerful
  Neural Engine but keeps third-party
  access tighter. Qualcomm has strong NPUs
  that often want a vendor SDK. Google's
  bet is "same company owns the stairs."

  WHAT A LOCAL AGENT LOOP LOOKS LIKE
  1. You ask for something concrete.
  2. On-device model returns a structured
     action (not just a paragraph).
  3. The app validates the action.
  4. If the next step needs the network
     (calendar write, send, fetch), it
     queues until connectivity exists.
  5. Status comes back: done, failed, or
     waiting.

  That queue step is the unglamorous hero.
  Offline brains still live in an online
  world. Good agents remember unfinished
  work instead of pretending the sky is
  always clear.

  LIMITS WORTH RESPECTING
  - Local models hallucinate tool names
    more than big cloud models when schemas
    get complex. Validate everything.
  - RAM matters. Gemini Intelligence class
    features are being tied to higher RAM
    floors (think 12GB-class devices, with
    16GB happier for heavier local loads).
  - "Agentic" in a keynote still means
    "narrow tools done well," not a digital
    employee with judgment.

  FIELD NOTE FOR MARKO
  If you only remember one midweek idea:
  the industry is racing to put a small,
  trustworthy planner in the device itself.
  Cloud models remain the heavy thinkers.
  Phones are becoming the hands.

  WHAT IS NEW ACROSS THE FAMILY
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

  TENSOR G6
  Google calls it the fastest Pixel chip
  yet. Compared with Tensor G5 at launch,
  Google claims about 25 percent faster
  web browsing and 15 percent quicker app
  launches. The TPU side is the bigger
  story for AI: Google says about 50
  percent more TPU compute, with on-device
  AI tasks up to 3.5 times faster while
  using up to 3.5 times less energy. The
  chip is built to run the latest Gemini
  Nano model on the phone itself.

  GEMINI INTELLIGENCE
  The whole line is framed around more
  proactive help: Gemini Live, Gemini
  apps, Magic Cue, Circle to Search,
  Live Translate, and Call Assist. Some
  features are country and language
  limited. Some need a subscription for
  higher usage. Pro purchases include six
  months of Google AI Pro (higher Gemini
  usage, 5TB cloud storage, Google Health
  Premium).

  CHARGING AND STORAGE FLOOR
  Every model starts at 256GB. Pixelsnap
  is built in. The line moves to Qi 2.2
  wireless charging up to 25W. Wired
  speed still depends on the charger you
  actually own (sold separately).

  DURABILITY AND UPDATES
  IP68 dust and water resistance design
  rating when each device leaves the
  factory (not permanent; wear, drops,
  and repairs can reduce it). Fingerprint
  Unlock and Face Unlock. USB-C 3.2.
  Stereo speakers. Three microphones.
  Seven years of OS, security, and Pixel
  Drop updates from U.S. Google Store
  availability.

  CAMERA BAR
  Edge-to-edge glass across the iconic
  Pixel camera bar. On the standard Pixel
  11, Google says the bar is about 40
  percent thinner than earlier base Pixel
  generations, which cuts table wobble
  when the phone is face up.

  This is not a small refresh day. Google
  widened the standard-versus-Pro gap on
  purpose: brighter Pro glass, HiLight,
  heavier telephoto hardware, and a Fold
  that stays in the family while the slab
  Pros sprint on zoom.

  For a reader who cares about cameras
  first, the decision tree is blunt:
  Pixel 11 for excellent daily photos at
  the lower price; Pro or Pro XL if zoom
  and Pro controls are the point; Fold if
  the screen shape is the point.

  Pre-order: today.
  In stores: August 20.
  The rest is preference, pocket size,
  and how much glass you want between
  your thumbs.
